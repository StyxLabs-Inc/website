## 5. Content Style & Tone Guidelines

1. **Avoid Generic AI Clichés:** Skip overused buzzwords like "game-changing," "revolutionary," or "magic" without supporting technical details.
2. **Emphasize Action & Systems:** Use verbs like *orchestrate, deploy, scale, architect, optimize,* and *synthesize*.
3. **Target Audience:** Enterprise CTOs, VPs of AI/Engineering, Product Leaders, and Innovation Directors.

---

## 6. How to Use This Skill

When prompting the assistant for website copy, specify the target page or section:
- `"Generate the 'About Us' section for StyxLabs using SKILL.md"`
- `"Draft a product feature landing page for StyxLabs Multi-Agent Platform"`
- `"Write call-to-action cards for StyxLabs solutions page"`
