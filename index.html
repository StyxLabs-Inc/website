How It Works

From request to result, fully orchestrated.

  1. 1

    Understand

    An orchestrator agent interprets the goal and breaks it into well-scoped tasks.

  2. 2

    Delegate

    Specialist agents — research, analysis, retrieval, action — take each task in parallel.

  3. 3

    Act

    Agents call your systems through governed tool connectors, within the permissions you set.

  4. 4

    Verify

    Reviewer agents and policy checks validate outputs before anything is committed.

  5. 5

    Report

    Every step is logged, traceable, and explainable to the humans accountable for it.
