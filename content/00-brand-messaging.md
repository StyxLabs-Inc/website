## Positioning Statement

**For** enterprise technology leaders who need AI that does real work inside real systems,
**StyxLabs** is an applied AI research and engineering company
**that** designs, builds, and operates agentic platforms and multi-agent systems.
**Unlike** consultancies that stop at prototypes or vendors that ship a single model behind an API,
**StyxLabs** carries work from research through to governed, production-scale deployment.

## Brand Narrative (the "Styx" story)

In myth, the Styx was the river at the boundary between two worlds. Few crossed it, and nothing came back unchanged.

For most enterprises, the gap between AI research and AI in production is that boundary. Pilots stall. Prototypes never meet a compliance review. Demos don't survive contact with legacy systems.

StyxLabs exists to make that crossing routine. Our mark — a flowing current wrapped around a circuit — reflects how we work: research that moves fluidly into engineered, dependable systems.

> **Short form:** *We take AI across the line — from research to production.*

## Voice & Tone

**We are:** authoritative, precise, systems-minded, quietly confident.
**We are not:** hype-driven, vague, cute, or breathless.

| Do | Don't |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Agents that decompose a request into tasks and verify each step." | "Revolutionary AI magic that changes everything." |
| "Deploy in your VPC with role-based access and full audit logs." | "Enterprise-grade, best-in-class, world-leading security." |
| Use verbs: *orchestrate, deploy, scale, architect, optimize, synthesize, verify* | Use: *game-changing, disruptive, magical, unleash, supercharge* |
| Name the mechanism, then the benefit. | Claim a benefit with no mechanism. |
| State numbers only when we can source them. | Invent metrics, customer counts, or "up to 10x" claims. |

**Audience:** Enterprise CTOs, VPs of AI/Engineering, Heads of Data, Product Leaders, Innovation Directors. Assume technical literacy; don't over-explain what an LLM is.
