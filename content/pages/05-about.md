## Our Story

In myth, the Styx was the river at the boundary between two worlds. Crossing it was rare, and no one came back unchanged.

For most organizations, the distance between AI research and AI in production feels the same. Breakthroughs appear in papers and demos every week. Yet inside the enterprise, pilots stall, prototypes fail security review, and promising models never touch a real workflow.

StyxLabs was founded to make that crossing routine. We bring research, analytics, generative AI, and agent engineering under one roof — so the people who understand the frontier are the same people who ship the system.

Our mark reflects it: a flowing current wrapped around a circuit. Fluid intelligence, engineered into dependable systems.

---

## What We Believe

#### Research must ship.
Insight that never reaches production is a cost, not an asset.

#### Reliability is the feature.
An agent that's impressive once and wrong the next time isn't ready. We engineer for consistency.

#### Autonomy needs accountability.
The more an agent can do, the more clearly you should see what it did. Governance is part of the design.

#### Systems beat single models.
The hardest problems are solved by well-orchestrated teams of specialized agents, not one larger prompt.

#### Capability should transfer.
We measure success by what your team can own and extend after we're done.
