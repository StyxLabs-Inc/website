## Research Philosophy

### Applied by design.

- **Production-relevant questions.** We study the problems that block real deployments: reliability, verification, cost, and control.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Every claim is backed by reproducible evaluation.
- **Short path to practice.** Findings flow directly into the StyxLabs platform and client systems.

## Research Areas

### Multi-Agent Coordination
How should agents divide work, share context, and resolve disagreements? We study orchestration topologies, communication protocols, and the trade-offs between autonomy and control.

### Reasoning & Verification
Methods that make agent outputs checkable — self-critique, reviewer agents, consensus strategies, and structured reasoning traces.

### Evaluation Science
Benchmarks and metrics for agentic behavior: task completion, tool-use accuracy, safety under adversarial inputs, and long-horizon reliability.

### Retrieval & Knowledge Grounding
Hybrid retrieval, knowledge graphs, and context management for agents operating over large, changing enterprise knowledge bases.

### Efficient Model Adaptation
Fine-tuning, distillation, and model routing to hit quality targets at a fraction of the cost and latency.

### Agent Safety & Governance
Permission models, policy enforcement, and audit mechanisms that keep autonomous systems within defined boundaries.
