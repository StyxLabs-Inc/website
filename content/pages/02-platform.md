## Architecture at a Glance

### Five layers. Each one replaceable. All of them governed.

| Layer | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Model Layer** | Connects to commercial, open-weight, and fine-tuned models. Route each task to the model that fits its cost, latency, and quality needs. |
| **Knowledge Layer** | Retrieval pipelines, vector and graph stores, and connectors that ground agents in your proprietary data. |
| **Agent Layer** | Reusable agent definitions — role, instructions, tools, memory, and permissions — versioned like code. |
| **Orchestration Layer** | Coordinates agents through sequential, parallel, hierarchical, and review-loop patterns. Handles state, retries, and hand-offs. |
| **Governance & Observability Layer** | Policy enforcement, human approvals, audit logs, tracing, evaluation, and cost tracking across everything above. |

## Capabilities

### Orchestration
**Coordinate many agents like one system.**
Define how agents divide work, share context, and hand off results. Mix deterministic workflow steps with autonomous reasoning where it adds value.

### Tool & System Integration
**Agents that act where work happens.**
Connect agents to APIs, databases, SaaS applications, and internal services through governed connectors with scoped credentials.

### Memory & Context
**Continuity across long-running work.**
Short-term working memory, long-term knowledge, and shared context between agents — with clear rules on what persists and who can read it.

### Human-in-the-Loop
**Autonomy where it's safe, approval where it matters.**
Insert review checkpoints on high-impact actions. Approvers see the agent's reasoning, inputs, and proposed action before anything executes.

### Evaluation & Quality
**Measure before you ship. Monitor after.**
Build evaluation sets from your real tasks, compare model and prompt changes side by side, and catch regressions before users do.

### Observability
**Every decision, traceable.**
Step-level traces of agent reasoning, tool calls, latency, and spend — searchable, exportable, and ready for audit.

### Security & Governance
**Controls your security team will sign off on.**
Role-based access, policy guardrails, data residency options, secrets management, and immutable audit trails.

## FAQ

**Which models does the platform support?**
The platform is model-agnostic. It works with leading commercial models, open-weight models, and models we fine-tune for you. You can route different tasks to different models.

**Do we need to replace our existing data infrastructure?**
No. The Knowledge Layer connects to your existing data warehouses, document stores, and applications.

**How do you prevent agents from taking unintended actions?**
Agents operate under scoped permissions, policy guardrails, and optional human approval on any action you designate. Every action is logged.

**Can we start small?**
Yes. Most engagements begin with a single high-value workflow, then expand to additional agents and teams on the same foundation.
