## Section 4 — How It Works

### From request to result, fully orchestrated.

1. **Understand** — An orchestrator agent interprets the goal and breaks it into well-scoped tasks.
2. **Delegate** — Specialist agents — research, analysis, retrieval, action — take each task in parallel.
3. **Act** — Agents call your systems through governed tool connectors, within the permissions you set.
4. **Verify** — Reviewer agents and policy checks validate outputs before anything is committed.
5. **Report** — Every step is logged, traceable, and explainable to the humans accountable for it.
