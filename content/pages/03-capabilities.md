## 04 · Agentic Platforms
`/capabilities/agentic-platforms`

### Agents that execute workflows end to end.

A chatbot answers a question. An agent finishes the job. We build autonomous digital agents that plan, use tools, and complete multi-step work inside your systems.

**What we do**
- **Agent design & engineering** — Define roles, tools, memory, and permissions for each agent.
- **Enterprise system integration** — Connect agents to the APIs and applications where work actually happens.
- **Human-in-the-loop workflows** — Approval gates for high-impact actions, with full reasoning visible to reviewers.
- **Operations & monitoring** — Tracing, quality evaluation, and cost controls for agents in production.

**Outcome:** Autonomous digital agents that execute complex workflows end to end — under your governance.

## 05 · Multi-Agentic System Design
`/capabilities/multi-agent-systems`

### Orchestrated agent collaboration at enterprise scale.

Some problems are too large or too varied for one agent. Multi-agent systems split the work among specialists — the way an effective team does.

**What we do**
- **Task decomposition** — Break complex goals into well-scoped units an agent can complete reliably.
- **Orchestration frameworks** — Hierarchical, parallel, and review-loop patterns tuned to each workload.
- **Inter-agent communication** — Structured protocols for sharing context, results, and hand-offs.
- **Verification & reasoning checks** — Reviewer agents and consensus patterns that catch errors before they propagate.

**Outcome:** Automate complex, multi-step business logic with self-coordinating AI agents.
